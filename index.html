07 · Workshop → Item → Advisor Plan

Pick a branch to see its total VAS target, the item quantities needed to hit it, and exactly how that splits across the advisors there — each using their own real package target, not a guess. Pandikkad and Ponnani are excluded — no VAS activity recorded there.
Built from item-mix history
Network fixed target: ₹1.5 Cr/month. Your 147 advisors' own chosen package targets already sum to ₹2.31 Cr (their combined MAX potential) — that's well above the ₹1.5 Cr floor. Scaling every advisor's target down by the same factor gives each of them a MIN to hit so the network lands exactly on ₹1.5 Cr, while their MAX stays their real personal target. This split naturally weights by how many advisors are at each branch and what packages they're on — no separate branch formula needed.
Method: this is a full implementation plan, not a gap-chase — current VAS figures aren't reliably monitored, so each advisor's entire target (MIN, MAX, or their saved Commitment) is allocated across all 16 catalog items in proportion to what their workshop's customers actually buy. Edit any row below, then "💾 Save as Commitment" to lock those numbers in as what that advisor has actually committed to — it becomes its own basis option, available everywhere this dashboard shows advisor targets. The branch total is always the exact sum of its advisors — so Network → Branch → Item → Advisor always adds up.

09 · Advisor & Workshop Planner

A working sheet, not just a report. Pick an advisor or a workshop, pick a VAS amount to target, and the planner suggests an item mix weighted by proven market potential (Section 08) — then you can change any item's quantity to match how that advisor or workshop actually sells, and watch the total update live. Hit "💾 Save" and this plan becomes their real Commitment — reflected instantly in Section 07, the Main page, and the Day-wise Planner.
Editable · market-weighted suggestions
How suggestions are built: for the selected target amount, each item gets a share based on that branch's market-potential mix from Section 08 (items with the biggest proven competitor gap get a bigger suggested share). Every quantity is then yours to change — every advisor has their own strengths, so the plan should match how they actually sell, as long as the total still hits the target.

Split a monthly VAS target across the days of the current month — Workshop-wise or Advisor-wise. A flat, equal split is the honest default here: there's no day-of-week sales pattern in your data to weight it by, so every working day carries the same share unless you edit a day directly.

Which one to use: the calendar is better for a workshop noticeboard or a quick glance in a review meeting. The table is more useful for day-to-day tracking — each day's target is independently editable (so you can put more on a day with a known big booking), it shows a running cumulative total, and it exports cleanly to compare against actuals. Start with the table for daily use; pull up the calendar for the big-picture view.
